Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Yaara Social ("Yaara", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Yaara mobile application and website (yaara.social). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, name, and username. This is required to identify you on the platform.

Profile Data: You may choose to provide additional information such as a bio, avatar photo, and location (city/pincode). This is optional and controlled by you.

Community & Social Data: We store your community memberships, friend connections (limited to 300), and activity within communities such as posts, votes, and event participation.

Messages: Your direct messages are encrypted end-to-end. We store encrypted message data on our servers but cannot read the contents of your messages.

Device Information: We collect basic device information (device model, OS version, unique device identifiers) for human verification purposes and to ensure one account per device. This does not include tracking across other apps.

Data sharing: None of the above data is shared with third parties for advertising, marketing, analytics, or any other purpose. Data is processed solely to provide and improve Yaara's services.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not sell your data to advertisers or any third parties
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not use your data for ad targeting — Yaara has no ads
  • We do not share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes
  • We do not use algorithmic profiling to manipulate what you see

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To verify you are a real human (Proof of Life Protocol)
  • To connect you with friends and communities
  • To deliver messages and notifications
  • To enable community features (events, voting, directories)
  • To improve and maintain the platform

Encryption & Security

All direct messages on Yaara are encrypted end-to-end. Each conversation uses its own encryption key. We cannot read your messages — not even if compelled by a third party, because we do not hold the decryption keys.

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your account data, including secure server infrastructure, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and regular security audits.

Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored on secure servers. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

When you delete your account:

  • Your profile and personal information are permanently deleted
  • Your messages remain encrypted and unreadable
  • Your community memberships and contributions are removed

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Children's Privacy

Yaara is not intended for children under the age of 18. In compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable laws, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly.

Compliance with Indian Law (DPDP Act 2023)

Yaara Social is an Indian company and complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act). In accordance with the Act:

  • We process your personal data only for the purposes stated in this policy and with your consent
  • You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or contacting us
  • We will notify the relevant authorities and affected users in the event of a data breach, as required by law
  • We do not process personal data of children (under 18) without verifiable parental consent
